# Veteran progression and party balance

Baseline #356: 170 deaths in the four 100-day Forge runs; 164 were Level 1–3. All twelve top-three survivors were healers. Maximum depths were 6/8/7/7 (seeds 7351/42/2026/9321), zero natural boss attempts. The old report accidentally printed generation-time base HP instead of effective HP for top survivors; this is corrected. It was a reporting error, not proof of over-healing.

## Corrections

- The real LIVE Ghoul pack now belongs to its authored site and generation. It no longer grows in number AND HP merely because a hero brings a companion. Floor 1 remains one; Floors 2–7 have one or two; Floors 8–9 have two or three, bounded by actual free spawn positions. Per-Ghoul solo HP, damage, loot probabilities and 60 Hz clock remain unchanged. The historical atomic test/replay simulator retains its original single-Ghoul pacing. A new encounter uses the new canonical options; no migration of in-progress pre-change encounters is provided.
- The voluntary co-located formation fills distinct class roles when possible. LIVE battle entry allocates the closer actual walkable slots to tanks and farther ones to healers, preserving the party's saved identity/order. No new collision space, forced roles or recruitment across unknown space. The existing unique boss geometry and gate are untouched.
- Needed gear and healing loot are retained by a compatible living member first; excess goods still go to the ordinary rotating sellable-finds inventory. Found herbs fill the existing supply target, not an unlimited stockpile. LIVE combat settlement deducts truly spent herbs before allocating new drops; chest settlement shares the same current supply count. No free healing or phantom sale.
- A fresh haul return requires crossing a new multiple of the existing needs-based threshold, rather than any new item while already above threshold. Health, combat retreat, and legitimate gear-purchase resupply retain priority. Unsold items are not deleted or magically sold. This changes return frequency, not loot capacity.
- Class survival by entrants, veterans, pass holders, sampled party composition and effective HP are reported. 22 existing 100-day cases remain, plus two 200-day Forge runs with the same scripted merchant rules. The merchant still caps materials; no real-world visual/physical acceptance is implied.

The larger role imbalance remains a hypothesis to measure: healers attack at range and get renewable innate spells each battle, whereas tanks and DPS must close distance. Do not nerf healer abilities or change class stats until the corrected party incentives and supply handling have been tested. Compare survival and depth jointly, not just boss attempts.

## Architecture review and first candidate results

The first 100-day Forge result for seed 42 had 97 deaths vs 45 in #356, including 25 deaths on Floor 5 vs one. Both 200-day runs ended without a natural boss attempt. Do not merge that candidate merely for passing units. The initial fixed-size policy unnecessarily guaranteed 2–3 Ghouls from Floor 5 (including solo explorers previously capped at two). Restore 1–2 in Floors 5–7; only Floors 8–9 have 2–3. This is a measurable revision, not a proven causal explanation. A committed shop return must not deliberately charge its pursuer when healthy: the existing threat policy now receives the real backtrack flag, also in the observer. Use legal gear checks in that policy.

The LIVE walker and read-only route preview now use one pure step policy, including actual Floor 1–9 topology and co-located party knowledge. No second navigator or committed forecast. The existing test scene readiness probe is reused by the single-portal smoke test after a null scene boot race. No alternate simulation or clock was added. The old atomic fixture mode and its single-Ghoul pacing are still historical test/validation debt, not another production owner; do not extend it.

Ordinary encounter preparation and the Combat Lab roster import now share `ordinaryHeroCombatAttributes` in the existing progression module. The Lab retains real legal equipment identity and reach; editable synthetic training values remain intentionally isolated from campaign state. The regression compares all three T2 roles against real ordinary encounter options, not merely displayed labels.

## Candidate after audit run 28: equipment and melee action economy

The corrected navigation/pack candidate reached Floor 9 in three of four 100-day Forge scenarios (7 hero records) but still recorded 347 combat deaths versus 170 in #356. All 11 living Level 5+ veterans were healers; none were tanks or DPS. No boss attempts occurred. See the PR audit comment for the matched evidence.

The player identified ranged kiting as a major advantage. Keep the healer's ranged attacks, dodge and finite spell budget. Instead, correct two ordinary melee defense problems: the ordinary windup reaction was early enough for the parry window to expire before contact; actors could also react to someone else's ordinary strike. Permit melee heroes to forfeit a pending windup or recovery to use an actual stamina-paid defense. No canceled strike lands damage. Equipped melee heroes gain small, bounded interruption resistance from their real weapon/armor; damage still applies and large hits still interrupt. A T1 sword and shield now grants two armor points, below its T2 counterpart. The signature boss poise and telegraph rules are not replaced. All changes reuse the existing group motor, gear catalog, actor phase clock and contact rules; no new save fields or healing items.

These are initial tuning hypotheses, not a claim of achieved balance. Rerun the same matrix; compare no-shop, T1, buyback and Forge modes so survival gains do not erase the merchant's influence. Audit ordinary solo and party survival, with no guaranteed wins or forced boss access. Do not merge on green CI alone.
