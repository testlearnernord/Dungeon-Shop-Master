# Navigation and expedition continuity

> **HISTORICAL EVIDENCE — NOT CURRENT REQUIREMENTS.** This report describes its original dated candidate, tests or provenance. Old version numbers, pending tasks and runtime claims are not present instructions. See [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and [Alpha v40 acceptance](ALPHA_V40_ACCEPTANCE.md) for current contracts.

Phase C of #294. Baseline #354: the four forge treatments reached Floors 6–7. Across Floors 3+, 63,399 of 64,505 sampled descent-route decisions lacked a personally walked route to the stair. This is an observation, not proof that all those moves were wasteful: discovering a hidden stair is legitimate.

This pass changes the existing walker, not its cadence or the dungeon layout:
- A co-located party uses the union of its real walked cells while routing to a committed staircase. A stair genuinely visible from any of those cells is eligible for a connected route across observed walkable cells. No fog is revealed into personal visited history and a hidden staircase does not steer the frontier search.
- Stair search uses a single shared nearest-frontier search rather than voting between individual knowledge sets; the renewable-site farming fallback cannot divert a committed transition or move a party away from an already reached stair.
- A healthy ordinary-floor explorer retains a prior descent through a nonurgent stay decision; injury, combat retreat and actual salvage return retain priority. Combat settlement now uses the existing party negotiation and actual surviving members' readiness rather than hard-coding the Floor-9 readiness flag to false.
- Add actual movement, new walked tile, reached stair, tactical diversion, observed route and blocked route observations to the existing audit. The individual walked-route count remains comparable to the baseline; the additional usable-route measure must not be confused with it.

No new clock, combat authority, saved fields, global hero knowledge, free healing, gear/stat buff, price change, loot change, stair relocation or portal shortcut. The pure regression walks all 36 seed/floor combinations; the existing long-run matrix is the actual acceptance gate. The tests do not assert natural boss success without completed campaign evidence. Salvage-return tuning and boss-party recruitment remain separate evidence-driven steps.

The subsequent expedition policy pass deliberately keeps the four-item return trigger in Floors 1–2 and for under-equipped, wounded or unprovisioned heroes. Healthy, fully equipped heroes with actual supplies in Floors 3+ carry six (careful/greedy), eight (ordinary), or ten (reckless) goods before a fresh haul triggers a trip. This is a selected balance hypothesis, not a new capacity limit. Stale unsold goods do not retrigger a return. Real retreat and low-health needs take priority. An existing backtrack also survives a combat victory.

On Floor 9, permitted, armed and healthy heroes voluntarily prefer similarly prepared companions and use only true current sight to approach visible peers. The lower stair provides a physical common landmark for solo seekers. A solo hero waits there only while enough day remains, then may resupply; only a genuine saved two/three-person party can actually cross. A waiting step consumes the normal world event, and any real tactical threat still overrides it. No pass, companion, equipment, location, loot or HP is manufactured. Completed boss campaigns do not keep recruiting for the cleared fight. Existing save fields and plan vocabulary are retained. Compare with the navigation-only matrix separately before deciding whether to retain the haul policy.

The navigation-only audit revealed increased casualties in Seeds 42 and 2026 (113 vs 44; 84 vs 57 in forge mode), despite better route utilization and more depth changes. Most deaths occurred on Floors 2–4 and all recorded deaths in those treatments were combat deaths. The former depth decision treated full HP alone as preparation, even with no weapon. This is a concrete risk-policy defect, but the aggregate reports do not prove it alone caused the casualty increase. The consolidated candidate requires real class-legal equipment for deeper progress (reckless healthy novices may still risk Floor 1→2), allows unarmed explorers to climb toward safer floors, and considers a paid starter-gear purchase after genuine rewards. It also removes voluntary unarmed monster hunting. Gear, prices, damage, encounter generation, and healing remain unchanged. Compare death counts and cohorts, not just maximum depth. Do not merge the navigation-only candidate independently of the survival regression review.

The audit summary also reports total entrants, dead share, initial-cohort survival, early-level deaths, upper-floor deaths and real legal equipment at death. These are descriptive cohort measures, not exposure-adjusted hazard estimates. A stable final roster size can conceal replacement churn.
