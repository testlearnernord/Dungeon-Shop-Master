# Alpha v45 — elective resupply and upgrade demand

Fresh v45 campaign only. No save migration. The prior two v44 45-day campaigns defeated the boss without a merchant permit; leave readiness and combat thresholds unchanged.

## Scope
- Canonical affordable class-compatible T1-to-T2 upgrade preference on real loot or an exhausted route, level 4+ and HP at least 65%, reserve one loaf's price if no owned recovery supplies.
- Last physical exit is saved; elective returns wait at least 7200 ticks. Urgent health and fresh-haul logic retains its previous priority. A ready boss party still prioritizes its expedition.
- When the unique boss is cleared, exhausted Floor-9 route and an actual haul can motivate a physical shop return. Do not synthesize a sale or use hidden shop stock.
- Current-format save checks, actual exit ownership, no early repeat, affordability/class/tier rules and post-boss return regression.
- Repeat the 45-day Forge campaigns at seeds 7351 and 42 using the same scripted storefront. Compare served customers, paid forged gear, material buyback, carried finds, shop/hero gold, boss outcome, return reason and deaths to [v44](ALPHA_V44_ACCEPTANCE.md).

Manual visual and ten-day physical acceptance [#294](https://github.com/testlearnernord/Dungeon-Shop-Master/issues/294) remains open. Check actual candidate CI and campaign evidence before merging.
